# Market and competitors

Date: 2026-11-16. Method: web search only. Figures come from mixed sources. Verify before public use.

## Assumption

Jurisdiction is Ontario, Canada (civil and Mareva injunction matters, plus criminal). Confirm this. Law changes by place.

## Who needs this

| Group | Pain | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Self-represented litigants (SRLs) | Cannot afford a lawyer. Must learn process alone. | StatCan: 58% of family law litigants self-represented in 2019/2020. Estimates of about 50% in civil cases. No single current national count exists. |
| Clients of lawyers | Lawyer has little time. Client does the legwork (emails, documents, timeline). | Founder's own experience. Needs interviews to confirm. |
| Lawyers and small firms | Too many pages, too little time. Need an organized file from the client. | Needs interviews to confirm. |

## Competitors and neighbours

| Name | What it is | Gap for us |
|---|---|---|
| Justice-Bot | AI legal navigation for self-represented Canadians. No independent review found. | Verify pricing and features by hand. Likely forms-focused, not evidence-focused. |

## The gap we can own

Almost nobody builds for the step between "I have a box of documents" and "my lawyer or expert can use them in one hour."

Our lane: evidence organization, timeline, discrepancy flags, expert triage, lawyer-ready package, plus discipline and preparation for SRLs.
