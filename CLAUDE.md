# Litigation Support

Website for litigation help and self-representation.

## Rules
- Stack is not chosen yet. Do not pick one before research and planning are done.
- Write research in `docs/research/`, plans in `docs/planning/`, choices in `docs/decisions/`.
- Show legal information, never legal advice. Add clear disclaimers in the UI.
- Cite sources for every legal claim. Name the jurisdiction.
- Never commit real client or case data. Use `private/` (git-ignored).
